    How this works

    Three kinds of ballots are still out. For each, the model estimates how many there are, where they are, and which way they lean, then counts them 200,000 different ways to see how often each candidate comes out ahead.

    • Ballots AP still expects (absentee and Election Day ballots not yet counted). Assumed to lean like the votes already counted in the same county, with room to break either way statewide.
    • Affidavit (provisional) ballots, cast when a voter's eligibility is questioned at the polls. About 8,000 to 17,000 statewide, roughly half accepted, leaning somewhat more Democratic than their county. Replaced with official county counts as clerks report them.
    • Late mail ballots postmarked by Nov. 3 and arriving by Nov. 10. Very few.
